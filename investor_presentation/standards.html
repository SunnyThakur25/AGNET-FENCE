07 / Standards alignmentAgentFence investor brief

Security demand is becoming a standards and operating-model issue.

NIST is emphasizing trusted, interoperable, and secure agent ecosystems—including identity and authorization—while OWASP provides an actionable risk framework for autonomous systems. AgentFence translates those expectations into action-level controls and evidence.
