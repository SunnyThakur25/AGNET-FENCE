08 / Market framingAgentFence investor brief

Market framing: a growing control-plane category.

The opportunity is not a single, speculative market-size number. It is the security and governance requirement created where enterprise agents gain authority to affect systems, data, and workflows.

Use precise language

Market capitalization is not an early-stage product metric.

Market capitalization describes a public company’s equity value. It does not define the addressable opportunity for AgentFence and should not be used as a proxy for product value.

The defensible commercial framing is the intersection of enterprise AI adoption with the control domains required to safely scale consequential agent actions.

No TAM, ARR, savings, or ROI number is stated here without a transparent methodology and customer validation.
