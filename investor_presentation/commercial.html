11 / Commercial model & roadmapAgentFence investor brief

Commercial model, expansion loop, and roadmap.

Build a durable control layer around real agent operations. Validate commercial assumptions with design partners before presenting pricing, savings, or ROI as fact.

Commercial hypothesis

Price for governed production authority.

Platform subscription plus governed-agent capacity, protected environments, action volume, evidence/export modules, and optional integration services.

Pricing, conversion, savings, and retention assumptions are design-partner validation items—not current claims.

Expansion loop

Land with one safe workflow. Expand through operational proof.

01

Wrap

Connect one cloud or browser agent to a low-blast-radius action.

02

Govern

Set boundaries, Data Guard rules, and approval thresholds.

03

Prove

Inspect action traces and export evidence for oversight.

04

Expand

Add agents, environments, targets, policy owners, and workflows.

Retention thesis: approved policies, safe integration patterns, audit history, and compliance evidence compound inside each customer environment.

Product horizon

From governed actions to a control fabric.

Now

Multi-tenant action governance, Data Guard, approvals, action observability, controlled OWASP assessment, evidence export, and disconnected-safe Vault integration.

Next

Customer-connected Vault AppRole activation, deeper target-system connectors, richer policy packs, and integrations with operational security workflows.

Extended

A durable enterprise fabric to authorize, observe, and prove agent actions across heterogeneous runtimes without exposing raw secrets to agents.

Design-partner test: wrap one real action, show allow/block/approval, and inspect its trace.

Vault live activation remains intentionally customer-controlled: it requires customer-supplied Vault address, AppRole ID, and secret ID through secure deployment configuration.
